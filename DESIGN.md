## Brand & Style

WeighGuard is an official enforcement and verification platform for legal metrology and consumer affairs inspectors. The visual design establishes uncompromising institutional authority, technical precision, and high-trust transparency.

The aesthetic is **Tactile Corporate & Civic Forensic**: combining deep forest greens and archival warm paper neutrals (`#fbf9f4`) with high-visibility amber and crimson alert cues. It rejects ephemeral consumer fluff in favor of structured data density, explicit cryptographic verification cues (ECDSA badges, live hash streams), and high-contrast audit readability for direct daylight field enforcement and low-connectivity environments.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in institutional trust and field audit clarity:
- **Primary (`#132a24`) & Primary Container (`#1b382b`)**: Deep pine/forest hues providing state-grade legitimacy and high contrast against parchment grounds.
- **Secondary (`#b45309`) & Warm Gold Accents (`#d97706`, `#fde68a`)**: High-visibility alert indicators for inspection windows, due dates, and operational badges.
- **Tonal Parchment Surfaces (`#fbf9f4` through `#dfd5c4`)**: Warmer than standard neutral grays, mitigating harsh field glare while keeping eye fatigue low.
- **Semantic Forensic Tiers**:
  - *Critical Red* (`#b91c1c` / container `#fee2e2` / text `#991b1b`): Expired verification, seal breaches, or statutory non-compliance.
  - *Attention Amber* (`#b45309` / container `#fef3c7` / text `#78350f`): Time-decay warnings, upcoming MPE drift recalibrations.
  - *Verified Green* (`#15803d` / container `#f0fdf4` / text `#14532d`): Cryptographically stamped and compliant scales.

## Typography

The typography pairs the utilitarian clarity of **Public Sans** for executive hierarchy and administrative prose with **JetBrains Mono** for forensic identifiers, calibration counts, cryptographic signatures, and legal section codes.

- **Public Sans**: Used for titles, subtitles, card headers, and narrative inspection notes. Its sturdy proportions ensure rapid scanning even under suboptimal lighting conditions.
- **JetBrains Mono**: Strictly applied to device serial numbers, hash verification chips, statutory document stamps, and dynamic metrics to reinforce instrument accuracy and prevent character misreading (e.g., distinguishing `0` and `O`, `1` and `I`).

## Layout & Spacing

The interface implements a mobile-first, vertical card-stream grid engineered for quick one-handed audit workflows:

- **Grid & Margins**: Base layout utilizes a fluid 1-column mobile model expanding to 2 or 3 columns on tablet/desktop viewports. Mobile lateral margins are pegged at `margin` (`1rem`), with interior component gaps governed by `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) and `space-md` (`0.75rem`).
- **Dense Tactical Rhythm**: Tight spacing preserves screen real estate, keeping forensic indicators, inspection status, and direct action triggers within immediate thumb reach.
- **Safe Area Insets**: Top app bar and bottom persistent navigation strictly account for hardware notches and gesture bars via `pt-safe` and `pb-safe`.

## Elevation & Depth

WeighGuard favors **Tonal Layering combined with crisp, low-contrast structural borders** (`border border-outline-variant`), avoiding heavy blurry drop shadows that wash out on outdoor screens:

- **Surfaces & Card Elevation**: Most interactive item containers sit at `surface-container-lowest` (`#ffffff`) with subtle `shadow-sm` and a hairline `#d8cfc0` border.
- **Floating Controls & App Bar**: Navigation bars and app headers use subtle glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur-xl` at 95% opacity) over `#fbf9f4` to maintain context while scrolling.
- **Inner Recesses**: Switchers and forensic metric boxes use recessed tones (`#10231e` or `surface-container`) with `shadow-inner` to simulate physical hardware toggles and digital instrumentation readouts.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a **Soft / Architectural (`1`)** shape language:
- Standard elements use subtle `0.125rem` to `0.25rem` (`rounded-lg`) borders.
- Interactive inspection cards and prominent summary pods use `0.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- Status pills, cryptographic state badges, and round utility buttons use `full` / `rounded-full` for distinct functional differentiation from structural content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid forest green (`bg-primary text-on-primary`) with subtle inset borders (`border-[#2d5241]`). High tap targets (minimum 40px height) with bold iconography.
- **Enforcement Action (Seize / Notice)**: Deep crimson (`bg-error text-on-error`) reserved strictly for statutory compliance intervention.
- **Secondary Action**: Warm surface button (`bg-surface-container-high border border-outline-variant text-primary`) with tactile feedback states (`active:scale-95`).

### Dual-Lens Switcher
Segmented hardware-style toggle housed in a dark container (`#10231e`). Active mode renders as an extruded pill (`bg-surface text-primary`), while inactive options remain subdued (`text-on-primary-container`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Monospaced Verification Tag**: `font-code-verification`, bold, uppercase, padding `px-1.5 py-0.5`, rounded. Color-coded strictly to semantic status (Critical, High Risk, Compliant).
- **Statutory Filter Pills**: Compact rounded-full pills combining icon + label for rapid category toggle across device class, zone sector, and priority ranking.

### Inspection Queue Cards
Composed in `surface-container-lowest` with a structured tripartite hierarchy:
1. **Header Row**: Risk confidence badge, device UID tag (`font-label-md text-secondary`), and class marker flanked by enforcement status pill.
2. **Body**: Primary device denomination with custodian APMC location, paired with a tinted forensic decay callout box (`bg-[#fef2f2]/60` or `bg-[#fffbeb]/70`).
3. **Metric / Evidence Grid**: Dual micro-stat boxes rendered in monospaced font displaying drift index, daily throughput, or totalizer hashes.
4. **Action Dock**: Integrated primary test button with secondary audit history trigger.

### Bottom Navigation Dock
Fixed elevation dock featuring labeled icon states. Active tab highlights in `text-secondary` (`#b45309`) with bold typography; inactive links utilize muted `text-on-surface-variant`.
